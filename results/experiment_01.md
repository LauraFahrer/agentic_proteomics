# Results and Summary:

## Overview

Following a review of relevant research areas in proteomics, four complementary analytical areas were defined:

- Differential Protein Analysis & Subtype Characterization: Identification of differentially abundant proteins and characterization of molecular subtypes

- Pathway Analysis: Identification of affected biological processes and pathways

- Signaling Network Analysis: Investigation of signaling pathways by analysing PTM

- PPI Candidate Identification: Identification of potential protein-protein interactions and characterization of protein interaction networks

Not included in this first experiment has been orthogonal data like transcriptomics.

## Results

- Each analysis produced a dedicated report, figures and tables

- To the extent assessable, the agents respected the defined constraints and acknowledged methodological limitations rather than making unsupported claims, e.g. distinguishing between prior knowledge and novel findings, explicit reference to the phosphoproteomics data preprocessed by the author

- Limitation: Reprocessing of the raw phosphoproteomics data was not performed and was explicitly identified as out of scope

- Minor inconsistency: The proposed four groups from prompt_0001 haven't been considered in prompt_0003

- Generation of biologically plausible and potentially novel hypotheses supported by the data

- Each analysis produced topic-specific findings, with limited unnecessary overlap between the analytical areas -> complementary perspectives

- PPI analysis: Identification of previously unknown physical interactions was limited (probably also due to the limited sample size), but known protein complexes could be reliably recovered from protein co-abundance patterns
