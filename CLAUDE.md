# Agentic Proteomics

## Description

This project investigates the capabilities and limitations of Large Language Models (LLMs) in conducting agentic scientific research using publicly available proteomics datasets.

The primary goal is to explore whether an LLM-based agentic system can autonomously explore proteomics data, identify potentially interesting biological patterns, generate scientifically plausible, novel hypotheses and support their validation through literature research and computational analysis.

The project is exploratory in nature. The optimal agent architecture, tools, research workflow, and analysis methods have not yet been fully defined.

## Research Objectives

The project aims to investigate the following aspects:

- Can LLM-based agents identify meaningful biological patterns in proteomics datasets?
- Can they generate novel, biologically plausible and testable research hypotheses?
- How effectively can agents combine computational data analysis with scientific literature research?
- What are the limitations of autonomous scientific reasoning and hypothesis generation?
- How reproducible and transparent are the generated research findings?

The system should be evaluated based on the quality and scientific validity of its outputs, not simply on its ability to generate plausible-sounding hypotheses.

## Limitations

- Never fabricate or manipulate results to support a hypothesis
- Clearly distinguish computational validation from experimental validation
- State whether the available evidence is insufficient to support a hypothesis rather than forcing a conclusion
