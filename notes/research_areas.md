# Current Research Areas

To get an overview about current research topics, I performed an agentic search asking for a summary list fo topics using proteomics and mass spectrometry data. This led to the following research areas:

- differential protein abundance between different experimental settings (e.g. healthy vs. disease, knockout vs. wildtype)

- differences between different celllines or molecular subtypes (e.g. comparing celltypes, celllines, tumor subtypes)

- pathway analysis -> interpret the changes in protein abundance in biological context (e.g. de/activation of pathways)

- signal transduction and phosphorylation (e.g. how a medication influence a signaling pathway, regulation of proteins, different phosphorylation patterns between healthy and disease)

- post-translational modifications -> affecting function, stability and PPI (e.g. acetylation, methylation, ubiquitinylation, glycosylation)

- drug-target interaction and mechanism of action (e.g. identifying protein targets of a medication, further effects on cell)

- PPI (e.g. composition of protein complexes, interaction networks, changes through medication)

- biomarker discovery (e.g. identifying proteins indicating a disease, its progression and the prognosis of a therapy)

- secretome and extracellular vesicle proteom (e.g. secreted proteins of a tumor cell, differences to a healthy control)
