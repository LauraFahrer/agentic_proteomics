## H1: The metabolism subtype is driven by a convergent, redundant receptor-tyrosine-kinase network feeding RAS-ERK

- 24 of 25 pY sites are higher in Metabolism subtype and located in RAS-ERK signaling pathway
- the actionable node of the pathway is MEK and paper found group sensitive to MEK-inhibitor (Trametinib, experimentally)
- findings: active and redundant Receptor Tyrosine Kinases (RTK) network belonging to RAS-ERK, possible explanation of sensitivity towards MEK-inhibitors

## H2: The EMT subtype runs a PI3K-AKT-mTOR program with a co-active, under-appreciated SGK arm

- increased PI3K-AKT-mTOR signaling, with both AKT and SGK contributing in EMT subtype
- SGK can activate mTOR independently of AKT -> may compensate AKT inhibition
- could explain why EMT cells respond to mTOR inhibition (Rapamycin) but not to AKT inhibition alone (experimentally)
